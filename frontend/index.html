How it works

From a unit to a study plan

Four steps. You choose the unit, the app does the sorting.

  1. Choose a unit.Select the subject and unit you want to be checked on.
  2. Take the diagnostic quiz.Choose 10 to 50 questions. They cover every topic in the unit.
  3. Read your results.See score, topic-by-topic accuracy, and the topics that need work.
  4. Close the gaps.Practise weak topics, follow the study plan, or ask the AI agent to explain.

Right now the question bank is sample data for Data Structures, units 1 and 2. AI-written reports and chat need an AI key on the server; without one, reports are labelled “Demo” and chat is unavailable.

        About

        About Study Gap Detector

        Why it exists

        Most revision starts with “I should study everything”. That is slow, and it hides the real problem: a few specific topics that were never fully understood. Study Gap Detector helps a student find those topics quickly and spend time on them first.

        Who it is for

        Engineering students (the question bank and syllabus format are built around B.Tech units) who want a fast, honest check on where they stand in a subject before an exam or before moving on to the next unit.

        What it does

        • Runs a diagnostic quiz on a chosen unit.
        • Marks every topic strong, weak, or not yet judged, using fixed, visible rules. The AI never decides a score.
        • Writes a study report and suggests curated resources for weak topics.
        • Lets you chat with an AI study agent that can read your saved results, the syllabus and files you attach.

        What to keep in mind

        • The question bank is currently sample data (Data Structures, units 1 and 2). It is not an official syllabus.
        • AI text can be wrong. Check it against your textbook and class notes.
        • Learning resource links may not have been checked yet; the app labels each one.
